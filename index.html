Hi, I'm Abhay Tripathi

Pixels have feelings — and I make sure they're happy. I'm Abhay, a UI/UX Designer who obsesses over the details everyone else scrolls past: the 2px gap, the exact shade of red, the button that just feels right to click. My job is turning "it's just an idea" into interfaces people actually enjoy using — not just look at. And because a good designer should never say "that's a developer problem," I bring my own designs to life with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — from e-commerce clones to a real attendance system that verifies you're really you using GPS and Face Recognition. Still early in the journey, still ridiculously curious, and always one Figma frame away from the next obsession.
